# Email Organizer Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Publisher: Aleksandr Prozorov

Privacy and support contact: Prozorov@live.com

This policy describes how Email Organizer handles information when you use the app.

## Information accessed

After you choose to connect an account, the app requests permission to read and manage mail. An Inbox scan reads sender addresses and message metadata to calculate sender counts. Message subjects and sender details are fetched when you open a preview. Full message content is fetched only when you open an individual message. When you browse your mailbox in Managed mail, the app lists your folder or label names and, for the folder you open, message senders, subjects, dates, and read status. Remote images are blocked by default and downloaded only after you tap “Load remote images.”

## Mailbox changes

When you explicitly confirm an action, the app can create provider rules, move messages to Trash or a folder, block or filter future messages, merge duplicate rules, and delete Spam or Junk. On Gmail, deleting Spam moves it to Trash, which Gmail empties automatically after 30 days; the app cannot empty Gmail Trash. On Outlook, deleting Junk or emptying Deleted Items permanently deletes messages. Bulk Inbox cleanup moves messages older than the selected period to Trash. From Spam or Junk you can move a message back to your Inbox. Your provider may retain trashed messages for a limited time; permanent deletion cannot be undone. Review every confirmation before continuing.

When you open a message in Managed mail, the app marks it as read in your mailbox, as a mail app would.

## Sending replies

For Gmail accounts, the app can send a reply that you write and tap Send for. The reply is sent from your Gmail account through the Gmail API to the original sender (or the Reply-To address), in the same conversation, and quotes the original message. The app never sends mail on its own or without your action. For Outlook accounts, Reply opens the message in Outlook; the app does not send Outlook mail.

## Google user data

Email Organizer’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

- Gmail data is used only to provide the features you see in the app: sender rankings, previews, reading, folder browsing, rules, cleanup, and replies you send.
- Gmail data is not transferred to anyone, except to Google itself to carry out the actions you request, or as required by law.
- Gmail data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build user profiles.
- No person reads your Gmail data. The publisher has no server and never receives it.
- Gmail data is not used to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

## Storage and sharing

Sender rankings and message previews are held in app memory and are not uploaded to an Email Organizer server. If you choose CSV export, a temporary file is created on-device and passed to the iOS share sheet for the destination you select; the temporary file is removed when sharing closes. OAuth refresh tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on this device. Mail requests go directly from your device to Google Gmail API or Microsoft Graph, whichever account you selected. Those providers process requests under their own privacy policies. The app includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, or data broker integrations.

## Purchases

Premium purchases, subscription status, and offer-code redemption are processed by Apple through StoreKit and are linked to your Apple Account. Email Organizer does not receive or store your payment details. Subscription management and refunds are handled by Apple.

## Security and retention

Connections use HTTPS and OAuth authorization-code flow with PKCE. The app does not ask for provider passwords. Disconnecting an account removes its saved refresh token from this device. Disconnecting Gmail also revokes the app’s access with Google. To revoke Microsoft access completely, also remove Email Organizer from your Microsoft account security settings. Rankings and previews are cleared when the app is closed.

## Your choices

You can decline account access, disconnect an account, or stop using the app at any time. You control mailbox changes and whether remote images are loaded. The app is not intended for children under 13.

## Third-party services

Email Organizer is an independent app and is not affiliated with Google or Microsoft. Account use is subject to the selected provider’s terms and privacy notices. Each person connects their own account through the provider’s sign-in and consent flow.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact Prozorov@live.com.
