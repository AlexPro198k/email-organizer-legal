# Email Organizer Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Publisher: Aleksandr Prozorov

Privacy and support contact: Prozorov@live.com

This policy describes how Email Organizer handles information when you use the app.

## Information accessed

After you choose to connect an account, the app requests permission to read and manage mail. An Inbox scan reads sender addresses and message metadata to calculate sender counts. Message subjects and sender details are fetched when you open a preview. Full message content is fetched only when you open an individual message. Remote images are blocked by default and downloaded only after you tap “Load remote images.”

## Mailbox changes

When you explicitly confirm an action, the app can create provider rules, move messages to Trash or a folder, block or filter future messages, merge duplicate rules, and permanently delete Spam or Trash. Bulk Inbox cleanup moves messages older than the selected period to Trash. Your provider may retain trashed messages for a limited time; permanent deletion cannot be undone. Review every confirmation before continuing.

## Storage and sharing

Sender rankings and message previews are held in app memory and are not uploaded to an Email Organizer server. If you choose CSV export, a temporary file is created on-device and passed to the iOS share sheet for the destination you select; the temporary file is removed when sharing closes. OAuth refresh tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on this device. Mail requests go directly from your device to Google Gmail API or Microsoft Graph, whichever account you selected. Those providers process requests under their own privacy policies. The app includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, or data broker integrations.

## Security and retention

Connections use HTTPS and OAuth authorization-code flow with PKCE. The app does not ask for provider passwords. Disconnecting an account removes its saved refresh token from this device; to revoke the app’s authorization completely, also remove Email Organizer from your Google or Microsoft account security settings. Rankings and previews are cleared when the app is closed.

## Your choices

You can decline account access, disconnect an account, or stop using the app at any time. You control mailbox changes and whether remote images are loaded. The app is not intended for children under 13.

## Third-party services

Email Organizer is an independent app and is not affiliated with Google or Microsoft. Account use is subject to the selected provider’s terms and privacy notices. Each person connects their own account through the provider’s sign-in and consent flow.
