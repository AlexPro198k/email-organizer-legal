# Email Organizer

Email Organizer is an independent iPhone app for reviewing sender activity and managing your own Gmail or Outlook mailbox. Sign-in is handled by Google or Microsoft. The app does not send your account password or mailbox data to an Email Organizer server.

## What the app does

- **Rank senders:** scan your Inbox to see who sends you the most mail, and export the ranking as CSV.
- **Clean up:** move a sender’s mail to Trash or a folder, block senders, and move Inbox mail older than a chosen date to Trash.
- **Rules:** create provider filter rules, get rule suggestions, and merge duplicate folder rules.
- **Spam and Junk:** review spam, return legitimate messages to your Inbox, or delete spam.
- **Managed mail:** browse your folders and labels, read messages, delete them, and reply (Gmail).

Every change to your mailbox happens only after you confirm it. Mail requests go directly from your iPhone to Google or Microsoft.
